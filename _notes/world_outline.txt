WORLD PASS: density, side contracts, reactivity, closing the gaps
================================================================
Binding spec for the content packages A-E. Every package owns its own files:
  data_atom/definitions/journal_<pkg>.rec, actors_<pkg>.rec, items_<pkg>.rec (loaded by glob),
  data_atom/dialogues/<new npc>.rec, data_atom/scripts/<pkg>_*.rec, data_atom/playtests/<pkg>_*.rec,
  data_atom/text/<pkg>_*.txt, and the map files (actors.rec/objects.rec/items.rec) of the maps it is assigned.
Shared files that may be edited: ONLY the ones named in the package. No engine changes without asking.
Every quest: fighter, avoider and manipulator route, at least one grey choice, consequences in the world,
no fail state except death, journal entries in a journal_<pkg>.rec. Every route gets a headless playtest.
Flags the engine sets for free: TalkedTo(npc), Killed(npc) (a counter per internal name),
KilledByPlayer(npc), ContainerRemoved(container, item), item PickupFlag, QuestCompleted(id, outcome),
QuestStarted(id), LocationKnown(map) via item PickupFlag or SetFlag, ebi_strike_landed etc. by scripts.
Reward money: PlayerAddGold(n) in dialogue effects; items: PlayerAddItem('name').
Time: scripts with SaveTimeNow('x') + IsHoursAfter('x', n)/IsDaysAfter. People: SpawnActor('def','map','loc'),
RemoveActor('internal_name'), MoveTo(actor,'map','loc'), RunScriptKill, SpawnTeamHunting(team,map,loc,victim),
ProvokeFaction('f'). Doors: a LockedDoor opens when its LockFlag game flag is cleared (ClearFlag).

LORE ANCHORS
- The dome. EBI (Eschaton Biotech) runs the "cleansing": the corpses at the southern wall are its work.
- Bushido-ArmaTech (BAT) sells weapons and "Meld" cyberware; Terra Vitae is a cult-like commune in the west.
- The golden ticket (1 million sat to the stars) is a scam; the spaceport ships leave empty.
- Logan Faust (mansion) sleeps in a cryo lab; E. (EBI) wants him silenced. Chapter 1-3 exist and work.

PACKAGE A: SIDE CONTRACTS SOUTH (maps: zone_residential_south, zone_residential_south_underground, zone_wall_south, hq_terra_vitae)
A1 "The Duck" (150 XP). Anna Whitaker's synth duck wandered into the sewers (move synth_duck to the underground
   among the rats; named location duck_pond). Fighter: shoot through rats, carry the duck back (duck follows when
   talked to: dialogue effect that uses Approach/follow or PickUp of a 'synth_duck' item placed when you talk to it;
   simplest: talking to the duck in the sewers gives item 'synth_duck_item', duck actor removed; giving it to Anna
   via StackTransferTo). Avoider: sneak past the rats. Manipulator: sell the duck to Big Bob for 300 instead
   (he wants the parts) and tell Anna it is dead (social Easy) -> she grieves (chatter changes), Bob's wares improve.
   Grey: Bob pays more than Anna.
A2 "Tags" (200 XP). Lucy (hq_terra_vitae, town_lucy gets a dialogue) lost her son in the cleansing. She wants his
   dog tag from the dead at the southern wall. Place item 'burton_dog_tag' at a corpse (items.rec of zone_wall_south)
   under the gate captain's eye: taking anything from the dead is a crime there. Fighter: kill the captain/guards.
   Avoider: sneak at night. Manipulator: bribe the captain (200 sat) or claim to be EBI records. Return the tag
   (she pays 100 and the Terra Vitae folks start greeting you) or sell it to Richard Shaw (zone_commerce, collector
   of cleansing relics, 400 sat) and lie to Lucy (social Hard; fail -> she knows, Terra Vitae turns cold: Father Olaf
   refuses to talk). Grey.
A3 "Rats" (100 XP). The cleaner (zone_residential_west) is also hired to clear the sewer rats but is afraid. He
   pays 150 sat for 6 dead rats (Killed(rat) >= 6 as a counter; govaluate: GetFlag('Killed(rat)') >= 6).
   Avoider/manipulator: buy rat poison from Big Bob (item 'rat_poison'), put it in the container 'sewer_trash'
   (script: IsItemInContainer -> after 2 hours ActorDie on every rat on that map via a script frame list) or talk
   the cleaner into doing his own job (intimidate Easy -> he goes down and dies to the rats after an hour,
   RunScriptKill style, and his widow... no: he simply dies; his body is found; the west loses its cleaner).
Reactive instances in A: R1 Anna grieves/thanks (chatter+dialogue), R2 Terra Vitae warms or cools to you,
R3 the cleaner dies and zone_residential_west gets dirtier over 2 days (CleanStains stops; a new sign
"Sanitation suspended" readable? objects cannot be added by script, so: spawn 2 more rats in the west after 2 days
via SpawnActor), R4 the wall: after killing the gate captain a new one (actor def gate_captain_2, harsher
dialogue, demands 50 sat toll to pass the gate) spawns after 1 day.

PACKAGE B: SIDE CONTRACTS COMMERCE & SPACEPORT (maps: zone_commerce, zone_spaceport, zone_industry)
B1 "Bar Tab" (150 XP). The bartender of La Boom (dialogue) wants Dec Vega's 400 sat debt. Fighter: beat it out of
   him (Kill or intimidate Hard). Avoider: Dec's stash is a container 'vega_stash' (medium lock) in the back.
   Manipulator: persuade (Easy) Dec to pay in instalments -> the bartender pays you half; or side with Dec, tell the
   bartender he is broke (social Hard) and Dec gives you 100 and a tip about the ticket scam. Grey: Dec is broke.
B2 "Dead Freight" (300 XP). Richard Shaw (collector, dialogue) wants a flight manifest from the spaceport
   terminal 'manifest_terminal' guarded by two sentry bots. Fighter: destroy the bots (EMP ammo exists? use what
   is in the game). Avoider: sneak. Manipulator: hack the 'spaceport_console' (technology Hard) to power the bots
   down (ClearFlag on a door flag is the only engine-level door effect; for bots use ActorSleep or RemoveActor via
   dialogue effect RunScript('b_bots_down')). The manifest (readable text spaceport_manifest.txt) proves the
   ships leave empty -> sets KnowsAbout(ticket_scam) (a second way to learn it). Shaw pays 500. Grey: sell it to
   Solara Carter instead (she sells tickets! give her a dialogue: ticket seller for the golden ticket) for 800
   and she buries it; the scam continues; Quest outcome 'buried'.
B3 "Shift Change" (200 XP). zone_industry: 14 EBI guards and nobody else. Add 3 'ebi_worker' and a foreman
   'foreman_okafor' (dialogue). He wants a stolen pallet of medical supplies (container 'stolen_pallet' in
   zone_commerce behind the gun_thugs) back without EBI noticing. Fighter: kill thugs. Avoider: lockpick at night.
   Manipulator: buy it off the thugs' leader (gun_thug leader gets dialogue; 300 sat) or sell the foreman out
   to the EBI officer in hq_ebi (ebi_security_officer gets dialogue; 400 sat; the foreman is removed from the
   world next day, replaced by 'foreman_ebi_loyal'). Grey.
Reactive in B: R5 Solara leaves town (RemoveActor) the day after the manifest is public (KnowsAbout(ticket_scam)
set via Shaw), a readable? no: her stall spot gets a 'gun_thug' squatter spawned. R6 the foreman replacement.
R7 Dec Vega dead -> the bartender's chatter mourns him and drinks cost more (vendor price flag if possible, else
chatter only).

PACKAGE D: CLOSING THE GAPS AT THE MANSION & LAB (maps: mansion, cryo_lab; dialogues logan_faust, mira_sykes,
mansion_guard, ark_terminal, cryo_pod, home_terminal, gate_captain; journal.rec recluse/cryo may be edited)
D1 Failed persuasion recovers: after persuade_faust fails (flag faust_persuade_failed set by the fail branch),
   Faust gives a condition: bring him the EBI gate log (item gate_log) or Mira's word. Mira's dialogue: do her a
   favour (the gate guards are skimming: option to confront the guards for her, intimidate Easy -> flag
   mira_favour) -> Faust's AlliedSuccess becomes reachable again (o_cond on faust_persuade_failed && (HasItem
   gate_log || mira_favour)).
D2 Warn Faust after selling him out: logan_faust option 'warn_faust' when faust_sold_out && !ebi_strike_landed:
   Faust and Mira flee to the lab (MoveTo both to cryo_lab 'pod_room', set flag faust_warned). The strike team
   finds the house empty and hunts the player instead (ebi_strike.rec frame branch). New recluse outcome
   'warned' (text: you sold him and then saved him; E. will not forget) placed BEFORE sold_out in file order.
   Faust in the lab: dialogue continues, he still gives the keycard if allied. Grey: E.'s money stays with you,
   EBI faction is provoked against you.
D3 Thief has a cost: cracking faust_safe sets flag 'safe_cracked' (ContainerRemoved(faust_safe, cryo_keycard)
   is free); a mansion script: IsHoursAfter of the removal -> Mira notices, the household is on alert: guards
   become aggressive to the player on sight (ProvokeFaction('faust')) unless Faust is allied. Journal line.
D4 Dead guards are seen: Faust and Mira get opening-branch lines when GetFlag('Killed(mansion_guard)') > 0
   (Faust: "You came through my people." -> persuade becomes Hard-only, extort becomes Easy). Mira when the
   gate captain is dead (Killed(gate_captain)): "The wall lost a captain today. Your work?" (yes -> she respects
   you, intimidate Easy; no -> nothing).
D5 Broadcast ending gets a cost: ARK says the broadcast draws the grid down; the sleepers in the other pods die
   (ending text in journal cryo 'against_all_odds' mentions it; set Killed(sleeper_1..3) before EndGame). Grey.
D6 Freeing a sleeper without killing: ark_terminal gets 'wake_sleeper' already; add 'move_sleeper' (technology
   Hard, needs cryo_fuse): move a sleeper's body to the spare pod 3 once fixed; both live. PodFree(1) without
   Killed(sleeper_1). Journal cryo outcome text for the clean path.
Reactive in D: R9 the household on alert; R10 Faust flees the house.
