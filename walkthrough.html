The Debt: walkthrough

Starter quest "Medical Extortion", plus the Chapter 1 hook. Tick the steps as you playtest; the ticks are saved in this browser.

Common start

  1. You start in the south residential district. Talk to Grim, take the apartment keys.
  2. Use the home terminal: (Job offer) Extract money, reward: 50% cut. Jacob wants 500 sat from Daniel Harker, the Ripperdoc at the hospital.
  3. Accept: you deposit 250 sat in the dead drop and keep the rest. Six game hours later the drop holds your reward (3× binge) and the quest completes.
  4. Ask Grim About that job for Jacob. How should I play it? for a hint at all three approaches (muscle, mouth, fingers).
  5. Ask Grim or Harker about Jacob to learn where he lives: eastern residential district, by taxi.
  6. When the quest is done, the home terminal shows (New job offer) A mansion outside the wall. Taking it ends the demo with your outcome on screen.

Not taking the job for 48 game hours counts as declining: Quinn Rix goes after Harker and the quest ends with 0 XP.

Fighter: take it by force

A. Rob Harker

  1. Accept the job, go to the hospital, talk to Harker: Do you know a guy named 'Jacob'? → I am here to collect the money he owes you.
  2. Money or violence. Choose. He hands over 500 sat (and is angry with you).
  3. Variant, after you've killed anyone: Maybe we can work something out. → That's not enough. → My means of violence are not limited by morality… → 1000 sat.
  4. Put 250 sat in the dead drop, wait 6 hours, collect the reward. Ending: reward_received, 250 XP.

B. Kill Harker

  1. Kill Harker, loot his gold (or open his stash), deposit 250 in the dead drop.
  2. Wait 2+ days: the clinic reopens with Drake (if you freed him and he lives) or an EBI medic.
  3. No Drake: the EBI medic walks from the EBI Headquarters to the clinic and calls the cleaner. The cleaner leaves his little room in Residential District West, scrubs every bloodstain in the hospital tile by tile (go and watch), then walks home.

C. Kill the client

  1. Get Jacob's location from Grim or Harker. Optional: Harker → What is your relationship with him? → I will take care of him. → We have a deal. (you now work for Harker).
  2. Taxi to the eastern district, talk to Jacob: You'll leave the Ripperdoc alone, is that clear? (intimidate, Hard). On failure, if you've already killed someone, his two goons outside join the fight.
  3. Kill Jacob. Ending: killed_client, or killed_client_for_daniel if you work for Harker. Return to Harker for 300 sat.
  4. Alternative: pay Quinn Rix 500 sat to do it. Get him talking about his guns → Let's say someone would drop some money… → I might have a job for you.

D. Drake

  1. Find Drake in the hospital cellar. I am your executioner. kills him; Harker reacts the next time you talk.

Diplomat: talk your way through

A. Make a deal with Harker

  1. Harker → collect the money → Maybe we can work something out. → Fine for me, but Jacob needs to see at least *some* money. He gives 250 sat, and you agree to keep him safe.
  2. Variant with Perception 7+: → That's not enough. → I can see a list of solvent clients on your terminal… → 1000 sat.
  3. Deposit 250 in the dead drop (or hand it to Jacob yourself: I'm here to collect my reward. → Sure, it's right here.). Ending: reward_received.

B. Squeeze the client

  1. Talk to Jacob: I'm here to renegotiate the terms of our contract.
  2. The risk was higher than expected. I want double. (social, Hard) or I know your dirty secrets… (intimidate, Easy). Success: 250 sat. Ending: client_extorted.

C. Make the client back off

  1. Wear a business outfit: I represent some bigger third party… (social, Easy unarmed / Hard with a visible weapon).
  2. Or intimidate: You'll leave the Ripperdoc alone, is that clear?
  3. Jacob calls off the job. Ending: client_backed_off. If you've killed someone, ask Got any real work for someone like me? to hear about the mansion.

D. Free Drake for nothing

  1. Cellar → So, who are you? → Stay calm. I will get you out. → You are welcome. He gives you the stash key and tells you about the stash.
  2. Alternatives: Nothing is free… → 200 sat, or a favor. Escort him to the secret ladder to free him.

Thief: nobody gets hurt, nobody gets paid twice

A. Rob the doctor's stash

  1. The stash is in the hospital's operating room, locked (easy). Holds 750 sat and 3× binge.
  2. Get a key from the filing cabinet, from Drake (free him, see Diplomat D), or pick the lock.
  3. Deposit 250 sat in the dead drop. Ending: reward_received.

B. Pay the client with his own money (best ending)

  1. Accept the job. Take a taxi to the eastern district; Jacob's apartment has a wall safe (medium lock, 250 sat).
  2. Pick it without being caught; his goons wait outside.
  3. Deposit that money in the dead drop. Ending: paid_with_clients_money, 500 XP.

C. Pay out of pocket

  1. Bring 250 sat from anywhere to the dead drop, without touching Harker. Ending: reward_received.

D. Walk away

  1. Decline the job (or leave it unanswered for 48 hours). Quinn Rix attacks Harker the next time you're in the south district. Ending: job_lost, 0 XP.
