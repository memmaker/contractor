The Debt: walkthrough

Starter quest "Medical Extortion", Chapters 1-3, nineteen side contracts and the living world. Tick the steps as you playtest; the ticks are saved in this browser.

Common start

  1. You start in the south residential district. Talk to Grim, take the apartment keys.
  2. Use the home terminal: (Job offer) Extract money, reward: 50% cut. Jacob wants 500 sat from Daniel Harker, the Ripperdoc at the hospital.
  3. Accept: you deposit 250 sat in the dead drop and keep the rest. Six game hours later the drop holds your reward (3× binge) and the quest completes.
  4. Ask Grim About that job for Jacob. How should I play it? for a hint at all three approaches (muscle, mouth, fingers).
  5. Ask Grim or Harker about Jacob to learn where he lives: eastern residential district, by taxi.
  6. When the quest is done, the home terminal shows (New job offer) A mansion outside the wall. Taking it starts Chapter 1 (tab "Chapters 1-3").

Not taking the job for 48 game hours counts as declining: Quinn Rix goes after Harker and the quest ends with 0 skill points.

Fighter: take it by force

A. Rob Harker

  1. Accept the job, go to the hospital, talk to Harker: Do you know a guy named 'Jacob'? → I am here to collect the money he owes you.
  2. Money or violence. Choose. He hands over 500 sat (and is angry with you).
  3. Variant, after you've killed anyone: Maybe we can work something out. → That's not enough. → My means of violence are not limited by morality… → 1000 sat.
  4. Put 250 sat in the dead drop, wait 6 hours, collect the reward. Ending: reward_received, 5 skill points.

B. Kill Harker

  1. Kill Harker, loot his gold (or open his stash), deposit 250 in the dead drop.
  2. Wait 2+ days: the clinic reopens with Drake (if you freed him and he lives) or an EBI medic.
  3. No Drake: the EBI medic walks from the EBI Headquarters to the clinic and calls the cleaner. The cleaner leaves his little room in Residential District West, scrubs every bloodstain in the hospital tile by tile (go and watch), then walks home.

C. Kill the client

  1. Get Jacob's location from Grim or Harker. Optional: Harker → What is your relationship with him? → I will take care of him. → We have a deal. (you now work for Harker).
  2. Taxi to the eastern district, talk to Jacob: You'll leave the Ripperdoc alone, is that clear? (intimidate, Hard). On failure, if you've already killed someone, his two goons outside join the fight.
  3. Kill Jacob. Ending: killed_client, or killed_client_for_daniel if you work for Harker. Return to Harker for 300 sat.
  4. Alternative: pay Quinn Rix 500 sat to do it. Get him talking about his guns → Let's say someone would drop some money… → I might have a job for you.

D. Drake

  1. Find Drake in the hospital cellar. I am your executioner. kills him; Harker reacts the next time you talk.

Diplomat: talk your way through

A. Make a deal with Harker

  1. Harker → collect the money → Maybe we can work something out. → Fine for me, but Jacob needs to see at least *some* money. He gives 250 sat, and you agree to keep him safe.
  2. Variant with Technology 40+: → That's not enough. → I can see a list of solvent clients on your terminal… → 1000 sat.
  3. Variant with BioChemistry 40+: → That's not enough. → Those vials behind you are unlicensed neural stims… → 1000 sat.
  4. Deposit 250 in the dead drop (or hand it to Jacob yourself: I'm here to collect my reward. → Sure, it's right here.). Ending: reward_received.

B. Squeeze the client

  1. Talk to Jacob: I'm here to renegotiate the terms of our contract.
  2. The risk was higher than expected. I want double. (social, Hard) or I know your dirty secrets… (intimidate, Easy). Success: 250 sat. Ending: client_extorted.

C. Make the client back off

  1. Wear a business outfit: I represent some bigger third party… (social, Easy unarmed / Hard with a visible weapon).
  2. Or intimidate: You'll leave the Ripperdoc alone, is that clear?
  3. Jacob calls off the job. Ending: client_backed_off. If you've killed someone, ask Got any real work for someone like me? to hear about the mansion.

D. Free Drake for nothing

  1. Cellar → So, who are you? → Stay calm. I will get you out. → You are welcome. He gives you the stash key and tells you about the stash.
  2. Alternatives: Nothing is free… → 200 sat, or a favor. Escort him to the secret ladder to free him.

Thief: nobody gets hurt, nobody gets paid twice

A. Rob the doctor's stash

  1. The stash is in the hospital's operating room, locked (easy). Holds 750 sat and 3× binge.
  2. Get a key from the filing cabinet, from Drake (free him, see Diplomat D), or pick the lock.
  3. Deposit 250 sat in the dead drop. Ending: reward_received.

B. Pay the client with his own money (best ending)

  1. Accept the job. Take a taxi to the eastern district; Jacob's apartment has a wall safe (medium lock, 250 sat).
  2. Pick it without being caught; his goons wait outside.
  3. Deposit that money in the dead drop. Ending: paid_with_clients_money, 10 skill points.

C. Pay out of pocket

  1. Bring 250 sat from anywhere to the dead drop, without touching Harker. Ending: reward_received.

D. Walk away

  1. Decline the job (or leave it unanswered for 48 hours). Quinn Rix attacks Harker the next time you're in the south district. Ending: job_lost, 0 skill points.

Chapters 1-3: the mansion and the long sleep

Logan Faust, ex Bushido-ArmaTech board, read EBI's "cleansing" plan and vanished into a mansion outside the wall with a private cryo lab underneath. The job to find him is signed "E.". The 1-million-sat ticket to the stars is a lie: the ships leave with cargo.

Chapter 1 · Find the mansion (The Mansion, 6 skill points, 1 perk point)

  1. Diplomat: taxi driver Ever drive anyone to a mansion outside the wall? → social Easy, or pay 200 sat. He drove the caterers out there.
  2. Fighter: the gate captain in the yard before the south wall gate: Trucks have been going out this gate at night. Where to? Intimidate Hard, or kill him and loot his gate log.
  3. Thief: Terra Vitae HQ, keypad door (0451 or hack it with e-picks), the invoice on the side office table.
  4. Taxi: The mansion outside the wall. (30 minutes).

Chapter 2 · The recluse (The Recluse, 10 skill points, 1 perk point)

  1. Gate: two guards. Claim to be the caterer or courier (social Easy), claim EBI (intimidate Hard), or Wrong answer. for a fight. A cleared gate unlocks the front door.
  2. Thief: the service door at the back (easy lock). Faust's wall safe in the study (hard lock): cryo keycard + 2000 sat. Hack Faust's terminal (technology Hard): opens the cellar door and reveals the lab; failure raises the alarm. Or point out the glycol ratio in his cryo notes would kill him (BioChemistry Hard): same alliance.
  3. Diplomat: Faust knows why you are here. I'm not here to kill you. → persuade (social Hard): you are allies, he hands you the keycard, tells you about the lab and the ticket scam, and offers his own pod.
  4. Extort: intimidate Easy: 2000 sat and the keycard, and he hates you.
  5. Fighter: shoot the gate guards. Gunfire carries: Mira Sykes, his bodyguard, and the hall guard come running, and the panicked flee into the garden. Mira carries the house key and the safe key. Kill Faust: his pod is nobody's now.
  6. Mira's round: every hour she walks hall, study, kitchen, gate. Time the service door for when she is at the gate, or meet her there with a gun.
  7. The journal follows you: ally with Faust and sell him out afterwards and The Recluse closes as the sell-out; if the troopers get him it closes as his death.
  8. Sell him out: Faust's terminal or the home terminal: Report Faust's location to E. 5000 sat. One hour later three EBI troopers land at the taxi stand and hunt Faust, then you.

Chapter 3 · The cryo lab (The Long Sleep, 20 skill points, 1 perk point)

  1. Cellar door (keycard or pick), stairs down. Four pods: 1 and 2 hold sleepers (Henrik Vale, Dr. Ilse Marrow), 3 is empty but EBI pulled its fuse, 4 is Faust's.
  2. Fighter: Pull the plug on a sleeper. Faust's people will not forgive it.
  3. Tech: the generator room (medium lock) has a spare cryo fuse for pod 3. Or ask ARK, the lab AI, to reroute power (technology Hard). BioChemistry (Hard, one try): skip the fuse and dose pod 3's cryoprotectant by hand.
  4. Diplomat: allied with Faust: pod 4 is yours. Or talk ARK into waking sleeper 1 early (social Hard).
  5. Climb in. One locker, one item. To be continued.

Side contracts

Every contract has a fighter, an avoider and a talker route, at least one grey choice, and no fail state except death. Each route has a headless playtest (data_atom/playtests/a_* … c_*).

A1 · The Duck (Anna Whitaker, south)

  1. Anna's synth duck Coco ran into the sewers. Old Abe the hermit knows: the pond by the east tunnel.
  2. Fighter: shoot through the rats (mind Bob's mines). Avoider: sneak past them. Talk to the duck to pick her up.
  3. Return her to Anna, or sell her to Big Bob for 300 (Anna pays less) and tell Anna she's dead (social): Anna grieves.

A2 · Tags (Lucy, Terra Vitae)

  1. Lucy lost her son Burton in the cleansing at the south wall. She wants his dog tag. Guard Ines tells you where he lies.
  2. Open the gate yard: kill the captain (his yard key), bribe him (200), bluff EBI records (social Hard), lift his yard key (stealth Medium), put the airlock in maintenance mode (technology Hard), or pick the yard door (easy). The airlock switch opens the dead field.
  3. Return the tag: 100 sat, Terra Vitae warms to you, Father Olaf gives you a gift.
  4. Or sell it: Richard Shaw pays 400, Mott the scavenger 60. Lie to Lucy (social Hard): fail and Terra Vitae turns cold, Olaf refuses you.

A3 · Rats (the cleaner, west)

  1. The cleaner pays 150 for clearing the sewer rats.
  2. Fighter: kill five rats (west tunnel). Avoider: buy rat poison from Big Bob and drop it in the reeking trash heap: two hours later every rat is dead (Abe won't forgive you). BioChemistry (Medium): offer the cleaner to brew a repellent; pour the bucket on the trash heap and two hours later the rats have moved deeper. Nobody dies, Abe approves.
  3. Grey: intimidate the cleaner into doing it himself. He goes down and doesn't come back; his badge turns up in the trash. Two days later the west fills with rats.

B1 · Bar Tab (La Boom)

  1. The bartender wants Dec Vega's 400 sat. Kill him or intimidate him (Hard); or lift Dec's stash (medium lock, back table). Or read his yellow eyes (BioChemistry Medium): scared for his liver, he hands over the wallet.
  2. Talk Dec into instalments (half for you), or tell the bartender he's broke (social Hard): Dec tips you about the ticket scam.

B2 · Dead Freight (Richard Shaw, commerce)

  1. Shaw wants the flight manifest from the spaceport terminal. Two dock sentries; one optic is cracked (Clerk Wen will tell you).
  2. Destroy the bots, sneak past them, or hack them down. The manifest proves the ships leave with cargo only.
  3. Shaw makes it public (500), or Solara Carter buries it (800) and the lottery goes on.

B3 · Shift Change (Foreman Okafor, industry)

  1. A pallet of medical supplies went to the gun thugs in commerce. Kill them, pick the pallet at night, or buy it from their leader (300). Or tell the leader the meds spoiled in the sun (BioChemistry Medium, one try): he gives you the key for free.
  2. Grey: sell Okafor out to the EBI officer (400): next day an EBI-loyal foreman replaces him and Pell counts his breaks.

C · Lost Badge, Silence, Rent

  • Lost Badge (corporate): Benny the tout has Chief Landry's badge. Kill, buy (200), persuade, or lift it mid-pitch (stealth Medium, one try). Return it and the BAT door opens for you; keep it and get in yourself.
  • Silence (EBI campus): Voss wants protester Ines gone. Kill her, pay her off, move her to the BAT campus (social Hard), send her to the Terra Vitae clinic for her chlorine-scarred lungs (BioChemistry Medium, one try), or warn her.
  • Rent (west): widow Marla owes Rook the pawnbroker. Pay 150, intimidate Rook, have her sell her husband's watch, or tear her page out of Rook's ledger (stealth Medium, one try).

F · Ten more contracts

Each one has its own shape, after the source adventures.

Every side contract (A, B, C and F) also shows up on the terminal in your apartment as a vague job offer naming who to ask. The offer disappears once you take the job.

  • The Slow Boat (investigation, then an ambush): Kade in commerce. The courier Hiro lies dead on the east side with a locker key. The chip is in the commerce left-luggage locker (or pull the locker code off the freighter manifest on Kade's terminal, technology Hard, one try), and 20 minutes after you take it, two Chiba gunmen hunt you there. Sell it to Kade (400), Shaw (600) or Father Olaf (Terra Vitae warms to you).
  • Sweet Revenge (the client is the victim): Shani at Terra Vitae wants Stromberg in corporate brought down. Crack his safe for the ledger (exposed), kill him, make him free her contract (social Hard), or sell Shani back to him for 800. Six hours later she's gone.
  • The '94 Harley (collect three parts): Dizzy in the south. The ignition comes from Ratchet in the west (150, or social Easy), the fuel cell from a burned car in the west (easy lock), and the tank from Mott at the wall (100). Then a 6-hour rebuild. Grey: Ratchet buys all three parts for 450.
  • Ecto (night stakeout): Lou in commerce. The ghost only shows after 22:00. Follow its cable to the junction box and kill the power, talk back through the projector (technology Easy) so Pixel stops, or take Pixel's 200 and lie to Lou.
  • False Papers (courier with a wait): Amira in the south gives you a photo and 100 sat. Nils in the west wants 250 (or intimidate Easy) and twelve hours. Bring her the card and she gives you her ring. Grey: Nils buys the photo for EBI (300), and the next day Amira's corner is empty.
  • Payback (information only): Stripe in the east pays for where Pinky hides. Suki the noodle vendor knows, or find him at the wall yourself. Tell Stripe and Pinky is dead by morning, off-screen. Or kill Pinky yourself (400), or warn him (he pays 180 and runs) and lie to Stripe (social Easy).
  • Carmen (evidence, two of three): Det. Rourke in the west. Take the ribbon off the victim behind the pawnshop, get Tito the stagehand to talk (social Easy or 50 sat), crack the dressing-room trunk for the diary (hard lock), or tell Rourke the wounds are a trained hand with a scalpel (BioChemistry Medium, one try). Any two get her arrested. Or confront her (fight), or take her 500.
  • Proxy Vote (two-day deadline): Carleton in corporate needs Hal's share vote. Fix Hal's robot dog with parts from the industry tool crate, have Nils forge the signature (200), or let Hal give his vote to Rhee's food co-op. Miss the deadline and Carleton loses.
  • A Holiday on Ice (the client betrays you): Nueman in commerce wants Morrow's specs. Bluff Morrow (technology Hard) or crack his briefcase. Deliver and Nueman's cleaners hunt you 30 minutes later. Ask Morrow about Nueman first, then take insurance (intimidate Easy, 800, no cleaners). Or give the specs back to Morrow.
  • Deadline (the rescued may refuse): Caitlin in corporate, with 300 up front. Free Holly Jones from Fargo in commerce: pay 400, intimidate Hard, threaten his books (technology Hard), lift the marker while he counts (stealth Medium), or fight Fargo and his two leg-breakers. Holly decides for herself whether to go back on air (350) or stay (150).

Where the gear is

  • You start with a trenchcoat, 150 sat, 3 lockpicks, a watch, and a binge.
  • Big Bob sells the 10mm SMG, JHP and AP ammo, and rat poison. His truck is stuck at the north checkpoint: bluff it through (social Medium), forge a permit (technology Medium), or pay 100, and his shelves fill up; after that you can also buy the SMG (500) and a box of JHP (50) straight across the counter. Harker refers you to Dr. Winters at Terra Vitae for major cyberware. Door codes: Grim Beard tells you your home keypad (0451); Nova Starling gives you Terra Vitae's (offices 0451, bar back rooms 1996). The pawnbroker and the Project 21 toolbox have electronic lockpicks.
  • The hospital filing cabinet holds a one-shot tranquilizer pistol. The Project 21 sysop crate holds the bowel disruptor and fusion cells. Your desk holds sunglasses and two lockpicks.
  • The bartender sells binge, brainz, buff and boost. Mott sells water at the wall. Suki sells noodles in the east.

Every map has people

Each map has at least three people with something to say, two things to read, and two containers worth opening (the cryo lab has ARK and the sleepers instead). The thinnest maps got Ines and Mott at the wall, Pell and a line worker in industry, Suki in the east, Old Abe in the sewer, and Wen, Tamsin and Maren at the spaceport.
